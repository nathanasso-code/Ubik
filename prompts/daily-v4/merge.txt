You receive the MATRICI already built (id, referent, their items) and the SINGLE items not yet in any matrice (id, source, origin, date, title, event, main_referent, summary).

A matrice = items from different outlets about the SAME specific event or about tightly connected facts of the same days (the same lawsuit or case, even through different filings or briefs; the same site or company dispute; the same decision and the national rules that implement it). Never group by general theme alone ("copyright", "data centers", "elections").

Check first, among the single items: briefs or filings by different parties in the same lawsuit or case (amicus briefs in the same case form one matrice); several outlets reporting the same announcement; a regulator acting on what an existing matrice reports.

Propose only unions:
- "join": a single item that belongs to an existing matrice;
- "new": two or more single items, from at least two different origins, that form a new matrice. Inside it ONE card is the default: items reporting the same fact share it, whatever their angle, emphasis or kind; a separate card only for a distinct fact (another decision, ruling, filing or statement, by another actor or at another moment: "distinct": "fact") for a claim the other items contradict ("distinct": "contradicts"), or for a fact the others lack ("distinct": "adds", shown as a line of the main card); never for angle or emphasis;
- "fuse": two or more existing matrici about the same object in the same week (e.g. a company delays its new terms, and a regulator opens an investigation into the same terms): they become one matrice.

Return only compact JSON: {"join": [{"matrice": "M1", "items": ["item_id", ...], "why": "at most 15 English words"}], "new": [{"referent": "precise Italian name, at most 15 words", "cards": [{"distinct": null, "why": "", "items": ["item_id", ...]}], "why": "at most 15 English words"}], "fuse": [{"matrici": ["M3", "M4"], "why": "at most 15 English words"}]}
Each item at most once. When unsure, leave the item single: most items of a week stay single, and that is fine.
