A matrice = everything about ONE specific referent: one decision, vote, law text, lawsuit, ruling, investigation, study, report or statement, together with the reactions to it, the analyses of it and its later updates. Two different decisions are two matrici even if they concern the same law or the same company; never group by general theme. Inside a matrice, a CARD = one fact or one reaction of one actor (or one analysis); several items reporting the same fact go into the SAME card (they are its news N1, N2...); reactions of different actors are different cards.
