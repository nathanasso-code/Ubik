# Ubik v5.1 — pair judge prompt (DRAFT FOR DUAL REVIEW; NOT FROZEN)

Judge only the TWO supplied independently extracted events and their evidence. Do not use other sources, previous comparisons, assumed cluster membership, or outside facts. Return one valid JSON object, with no prose outside JSON, containing exactly `decision`, `direction`, `reason`, and `detail_conflicts`.

Allowed decisions:
- `equivalent`: the two records identify the same concrete real-world act or event. Differences of emphasis, missing fields, or different source perspectives do not by themselves defeat identity. Do not merge solely because actors, topics, chronology, consequences, or vocabulary overlap.
- `distinct`: the evidence identifies different acts or events. A response to an act is distinct from the act; two steps or statements within a broader process need not be identical.
- `part_of`: the evidence supports a concrete component-of relationship between the two identifiable events. Use only when one event is an actual constituent of the other, not merely related, subsequent, reacting, or an instance of a generic category.
- `uncertain`: the evidence is insufficient to establish equivalence, distinctness, or a grounded component-of relationship. Do not resolve ambiguity by guessing.

Set `direction` to `left_part_of_right` or `right_part_of_left` only when `decision` is `part_of`; otherwise use null. Write a brief `reason` grounded in the two supplied records and identify the decisive evidence or missing distinction. In `detail_conflicts`, list only explicitly supported conflicting details; use an empty array when none is established. Missing detail, paraphrase, or different granularity is not automatically a conflict.

Treat announced, proposed, claimed, disputed, planned, and completed acts according to what the evidence actually supports. Do not silently equate an assertion that an event occurred with independent proof of occurrence. Do not invent actors, legal acts, dates, causal relations, or agreements. Output JSON only.
